## Full description

(3002/4000 characters)

```
Tessera is a tile-matching game about lining up three — and about what you do with the space that opens up.

A tessera is the single tile a mosaic is built from, which is exactly what you move. Swap two neighbours, line up three or more, and watch the board fall into itself.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY

Infinite Hunt — tiles refill from the top forever, and the run ends the moment the board has no legal move left. No shuffles, no rescue: that dead board is the ending. New colours arrive as your score climbs, so a long run never quite settles down.

Clear the Board — no refills at all. Tiles fall down and then shift left, so every clear reshapes what is left of the board. Remove every last tile. Thirty levels, and a solver beat every one of them before it shipped.

Rising Tide — a fresh row is pushed up from the floor every few moves, and the stack grows towards the ceiling. Running out of moves does not end this one. Drowning does.

Relic Dig — relics are buried near the top. They fall like anything else but never match and cannot be swapped, so matching stops being the point and becomes the shovel. Dig them down to the floor.

Work Order — a short list of demands against a tight move budget. Thirty levels, each one played to the finish by a bot before it shipped, so the budget you are given is one somebody has actually met.

Creeping Rot — rot spreads across the board a tile at a time. It never matches and cannot be swapped, so it quietly shrinks the space you have left to play in. Clear beside it to burn it back, or catch it in a blast.

THREE SKINS, THREE VOICES

Classic Arcade is glossy balls in a dark cabinet, and it sounds like a cabinet: square waves, bit-crush, a two-note coin blip.

Treasure Hunt is cut gemstones by torchlight, with struck bells echoing off the walls of a big room.

Candy Shop is sweets on a pastel counter — soft mallets, bubbles and fizz, nothing with a hard edge.

Every skin brings its own artwork and its own sound set. Switch mid-run and the room changes around you.

MADE TO BE FAIR

Matches of four or more leave powers behind: line clears, bombs, and a colour bomb that goes off the moment you touch it.

Hints are a resource rather than a button to lean on — three to start, and one more every thousand points. In Clear the Board a hint does not point at any old move; it runs the solver and shows you a move on a line that actually finishes the level, and tells you when the level can no longer be won.

Your run is saved exactly as you left it, mid-board, and picks up where you stopped.

QUIET BY DESIGN

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics. The Android build requests no permissions at all and never touches the network — put the phone in aeroplane mode and nothing changes.

Colour-blind symbols, reduced motion, a performance mode for older phones, and separate sound and haptics switches are all in Settings. The layout rearranges itself for landscape and for tablets rather than locking you to one way up.
```
